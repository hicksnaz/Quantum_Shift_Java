Learn JavaScript the Simple Way

JS Quest is a small practice site that helps you review the basics of JavaScript. Instead of just reading notes, you answer short quiz questions and see your score as you go.

How This Site Works

  1. Go to the Game page and press Start Game.
  2. Answer the multiple-choice questions about JavaScript.
  3. Watch your score update and use hints if you get stuck.

Who Can Use JS Quest?

This site is mainly for beginners: students, teachers, and anyone who is just starting to learn JavaScript. It can be used in class, in a club, or just on your own.
